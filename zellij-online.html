What is Zellij.online?

From the maintainers of Zellij, the beloved terminal workspace, comes an online multiplayer service that works.

Share your terminal

Collaborative coding, pair programming, remote work, or live streaming to friends and followers. You decide who watches and who types.

Private by design

A privacy-focused, end-to-end encrypted experience that works in your browser, terminal or phone.

100% self-owned

Not funded by VCs or private equity. We don't sell your data. We don't add unsolicited AI features. We're here for you, because we love our users.
